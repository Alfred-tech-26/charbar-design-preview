CHEF DE CUISINE

Ricardo Rodriguez

From kitchens in Argentina to kitchens in Canada, Ricardo brings the traditions he knows to the ingredients grown and raised here. At charbar, the parrilla connects the two.

CO-CHEFS & CO-OWNERS

Connie & John

Connie DeSousa and John Jackson first worked together in Calgary in 1999. Kitchens abroad, a return home, and a shared belief in local food led them to charbar. Passing on their craft is part of the work.

BY THE BOW

The Simmons
Building.

A 1912 mattress factory on Calgary’s Riverwalk became a place for a different kind of gathering. In 2015, charbar opened its doors in the Simmons Building, with a wood-fired parrilla at the heart of the kitchen.

Argentina shapes the cooking. Alberta supplies so much of what goes over the fire. Our neighbours at Sidewalk Citizen make the bread. The room belongs to everyone who pulls up a chair.
