01 / MAIN FLOOR

At the heart of it.

A wraparound bar, an open kitchen and doors to the riverside patio. The main floor keeps your gathering close to the energy of the restaurant.

02 / SECOND FLOOR

A room of your own.

Overlooking the dining room, the second floor can be booked privately or divided for smaller groups. Two bars and dedicated washrooms keep everything close.

Enquire about a future seasonal gathering across the whole rooftop or in a semi-private section. Rooftop events have their own food arrangements; the regular patio is currently closed for the season.
